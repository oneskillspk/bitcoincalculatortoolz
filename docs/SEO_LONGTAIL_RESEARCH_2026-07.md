### Top 3 strategic opportunities

1. **Own the "power law" cluster** — already at pos 6 for `bitcoin power law`, pos 46 on category, only 1 SERP competitor with real authority (bitbo.io AS ~30). Push to pos 1–3 with a hub page + 4 supporting articles. Combined addressable volume: **~5,900/mo**.

### Authority note

Your AS of 8 means realistic ceiling for the next 6 months is KDI ≤ 30 keywords. Anything KDI 60+ (bitcoin calculator, bitcoin converter, fear and greed index) is 12–18 months out. The plan below reflects this: **90% of the roadmap targets KDI < 35.**

### 14.4 AEO (answer-engine optimization) rules
